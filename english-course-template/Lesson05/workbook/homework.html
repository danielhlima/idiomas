Additional project

Write an article about yourself

Use the article about a favourite day as inspiration. Write about your real life in clear, simple English.

  • Give your article a title, date, and a few tags.
  • Write 120-180 words about a typical or favourite day.
  • Include times, daily routine phrases, at least three frequency expressions, and at least two family or possession sentences.
  • Use sequencing words such as First, Then, and After that. Use at, in, on, and to where appropriate.
  • Send your file to your teacher for validation and correction.

Possible opening: My favourite day of the week is Saturday because it is the first day of the weekend.

Plan: title and introduction → morning → afternoon → evening → why you like the day.
