Work with your partner.

  1. Look only at your quadrant.
  2. Read the five statements.
  3. Decide which statements are correct or incorrect.
  4. Discuss your answers with your partner.
  5. Present complete corrections to the class.

How to answer

Incorrect statement

Number 3 is false. The coffee mug isn't under the desk. It's on the desk.

Another correction

The sentence says the headphones are under the table, but they aren't. They're on the table.

Correct statement

Number 2 is correct. The keys are on the brown bag.

Speaking idea

I think number 4 is correct. The large plant is behind the table.

Possessive Adjectives

Who does it belong to?

Subject pronoun → possessive adjective

Subject Pronoun Possessive Adjective Example
I → my My laptop is new.
you → your Your notebook is blue.
he → his His desk is clean.
she → her Her phone is black.
it → its Its screen is big.
we → our Our office is small.
they → their Their meeting is today.

Belonging

Possessive adjectives show who something belongs to.

Laura has a laptop. → Her laptop is black.

Thomas has a notebook. → His notebook is blue.

Quick connection

I am → my you are → your he is → his she is → her we are → our they are → their

1 Before the noun

A possessive adjective comes before the noun.

possessive adjective + noun

my laptop her phone their office

Showing possession with 's

Use 's after a person's name to show possession.

John's laptop = the laptop belongs to John.

Maria's phone = the phone belongs to Maria.

person + 's + thing

John + 's + laptop → John's laptop Maria + 's + phone → Maria's phone

John's laptop → the laptop belongs to John → John = he → his laptop

Maria's phone → the phone belongs to Maria → Maria = she → her phone

First: Who owns the thing? Then: choose the possessive adjective.

2 Who is the owner?

Look at the owner.

  • John's laptop → John → he → his laptop
  • Maria's laptop → Maria → she → her laptop
  • Laura and Thomas's office → they → their office

3 Subject pronoun ≠ possessive adjective

Use the subject pronoun for the person. Use the possessive adjective before a noun.

  • SHE is Brazilian.
  • HER company is Brazilian.
  • HE is a developer.
  • HIS laptop is new.
  • THEY are busy.
  • THEIR meeting is today.

The Mystery Bag

Work with your partner. Share information and discover who owns the bag.

How it works

  1. Work with your partner.
  2. Each student uses their own computer.
  3. Both students enter the SAME pair number.
  4. One student chooses STUDENT A.
  5. The other chooses STUDENT B.
  6. Do NOT look at your partner's screen.
  7. Ask and answer questions in English.
  8. Share information orally.
  9. Use the clues to discover who owns the mystery bag.
  10. Be ready to explain your answer.
