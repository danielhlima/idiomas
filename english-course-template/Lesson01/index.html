Personal Pronouns

Words we use instead of names and nouns.

Pronoun Portuguese Example

You você / vocês You are a visitor.
He ele He is Carlos.
She ela She is Emma.

Am a software developer. — Incorrect

Grammar

Full Forms and Contractions

Two natural ways to say the same thing

Full Form Contraction
I am I'm
You are You're
He is He's
She is She's
It is It's
We are We're
They are They're

Contractions are very common in spoken English and informal written English.

  • I am Daniel. / I'm Daniel.
  • She is a project manager. / She's a project manager.
  • He is from Spain. / He's from Spain.
  • We are colleagues. / We're colleagues.
  • They are at GlobalTech. / They're at GlobalTech.

Grammar

The Verb to Be — Negative

Add not after am, is or are.

Subject + Verb to Be + Not + Complement
Subject Negative Form
I am not
You are not
He is not
She is not
It is not
We are not
They are not

Use not after am, is or are to make a negative sentence.

  • I am not a visitor.
  • You are not late.
  • He is not British.
  • She is not an intern.
  • It is not an office.
  • We are not in the meeting room.
  • They are not managers.

Slide 18 / 28

Grammar

Negative Contractions

Short forms are common in spoken English.

Full Form Contraction
I am not I'm not
You are not You aren't
He is not He isn't
She is not She isn't
It is not It isn't
We are not We aren't
They are not They aren't

There is no standard contraction amn't. Use I'm not.

  • I am not an intern. / I'm not an intern.
  • You are not a visitor. / You aren't a visitor.
  • He is not American. / He isn't American.
  • She is not in Sales. / She isn't in Sales.
  • We are not late. / We aren't late.
  • They are not in the office. / They aren't in the office.

Grammar

Yes/No Questions and Short Answers

Use yes or no, then repeat the verb.

Affirmative

You are a visitor.

→

Question

Are you a visitor?

Verb to Be + Subject + Complement?

Yes Answers

  • Are you Brazilian?Yes, I am.
  • Is she a project manager?Yes, she is.
  • Are they colleagues?Yes, they are.

No Answers

  • Are you Spanish?No, I'm not.
  • Is he an intern?No, he isn't.
  • Are we in Sales?No, we aren't.

Use No

No answers the question.

Are you a visitor? No, I'm not.

Use Not

Not makes the verb negative.

I am not. / She is not. / They are not.

Do not answer only Yes or No. A short answer sounds more complete and natural.

Grammar

Personal and Work Questions

Use what, where and which in workplace introductions.

What

Use what for general information.

  • What’s your name? My name is Daniel.
  • What do you do? I’m a software developer.

Where

Use where for places.

  • Where are you from? I’m from Brazil.
  • Where do you work? I work at GlobalTech.

Which

Use which when choosing from a group.

  • Which department are you in? I’m in Sales.
  • Which company do you work for? I work for GlobalTech.
  • AWhat’s your name?
  • BMy name is Emma.
  • AWhat do you do?
  • BI’m a project manager.
  • AWhich department are you in?
  • BI’m in the IT department.

What’s is the contraction of What is. Learn “What do you do?” and “Where do you work?” as complete workplace questions now.

Communication

Meet a New Colleague

Situation

Imagine this situation:

Today is your first day at a new international company.

First, interview your partner.

Then, join another pair and introduce your partner to the other students.

Step 1

Interview Your Partner

Work in pairs.

Student A interviews Student B.

Then switch roles.

Ask and answer these questions:

  • What's your name?
  • What do you do?
  • Where do you work?
  • Which department are you in?
  • Where are you from?
  • What's your nationality?

Take short notes about your partner.

Step 2

Introduce Your Partner

Join another pair.

Introduce your partner to the other two students.

Do not read your notes if possible.

Speak naturally.
