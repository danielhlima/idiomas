Lesson 4

Present Simple — Affirmative

Talking about things we do

Grammar build

Build the rule step by step.

Stage 0 / 4

1 — Most subjects are easy

The verb does not change.

I work in São Paulo.

You work in São Paulo.

We work in São Paulo.

They work in São Paulo.

I / YOU / WE / THEY
↓
BASE VERB

The verb does not change.

2 — He, She and It are different

Add a final S.

He works in São Paulo.

She works in São Paulo.

It works well.

HE / SHE / IT
↓
VERB + S

work → works • speak → speaks • drink → drinks • like → likes

3 — Sometimes the ending changes

Usually +S. Sometimes +ES. Have becomes has.

Most verbs

work → works

speak → speaks

drink → drinks

like → likes

Add -ES

watch → watches

go → goes

Special case

have → has

4 — Look back

The subject decides the verb.

I drink coffee.I → base verb

Laura drinks coffee.Laura = she → verb + S

We work on computers.we → base verb

Thomas works on a computer.Thomas = he → verb + S

Laura and Thomas = THEY → Laura and Thomas work together.

Main rule

Remember this.

I / YOU / WE / THEY → WORK
HE / SHE / IT → WORKS

Present Simple — Negative

Talking about things we don't do

Grammar build

Build the negative form.

Stage 0 / 4

1 — I, You, We and They

Use don't + base verb.

I work in an office.

↓

I do not work in an office.

↓

I don't work in an office.

They don't speak Spanish.

DO + NOT → DON'T

I / YOU / WE / THEY -> DON'T + BASE VERB

2 — He, She and It

Use doesn't + base verb.

She works here.

↓

She does not work here.

↓

She doesn't work here.

He doesn't speak German.

DOES + NOT → DOESN'T

HE / SHE / IT -> DOESN'T + BASE VERB

Laura = SHE -> Laura doesn't work here.

Thomas = HE -> Thomas doesn't speak German.

Laura and Thomas = THEY -> Laura and Thomas don't work here.

3 — Where did the -s go?

The main verb goes back to base form.

She works here.

↓

She doesn't work here.

Incorrect She doesn't works here.

Correct She doesn't work here.

Incorrect Thomas doesn't speaks German.

Correct Thomas doesn't speak German.

Incorrect Laura doesn't has a meeting today.

Correct Laura doesn't have a meeting today.

In the negative, DOES carries the third-person form. The main verb goes back to the base form.

4 — The pattern

Compare affirmative and negative.

SubjectAffirmativeNegative
Iworkdon't work
Youworkdon't work
Heworksdoesn't work
Sheworksdoesn't work
Itworksdoesn't work
Weworkdon't work
Theyworkdon't work

She has a meeting today.
↓
She doesn't have a meeting today.

After don't / doesn't → base verb.

Main rule

Remember this.

I / YOU / WE / THEY→DON'T + VERB
HE / SHE / IT→DOESN'T + VERB
After DON'T / DOESN'T→BASE VERB

          Present Simple — Questions

          Questions with DO and DOES

          Grammar build

          Build yes / no questions.

          Stage 0 / 5

          1 — Questions with DO

          Use DO with I, you, we and they.

          You work here.

          ↓

          Do you work here?

          They speak English.

          ↓

          Do they speak English?

          DO + I / YOU / WE / THEY + BASE VERB?

          Do I need this? Do we have a meeting today?

          2 — Questions with DOES

          Use DOES with he, she and it.

          He works here.

          ↓

          Does he work here?

          Laura works in Madrid.

          ↓

          Does Laura work in Madrid?

          Laura = SHE -> Does Laura work here?

          Thomas = HE -> Does Thomas speak English?

          Laura + Thomas = THEY -> Do Laura and Thomas work here?

          One person -> DOES
          Two people -> DO

          3 — What happens to the verb?

          When DOES appears, use the base verb.

          She works here.

          She doesn't work here.

          Does she work here?

          Incorrect Does she works here?

          Correct Does she work here?

          Incorrect Does Thomas speaks English?

          Correct Does Thomas speak English?

          Incorrect Does Laura has a meeting today?

          Correct Does Laura have a meeting today?

          DOES / DOESN'T + BASE VERB

          4 — Short answers

          Short answers use do / don't / does / doesn't.

          Do you work in IT?

          Yes, I do.

          No, I don't.

          Does Laura work in Madrid?

          Yes, she does.

          No, she doesn't.

          Do Laura and Thomas speak English?

          Yes, they do.

          No, they don't.

          Short means short.

          Use: Yes, she does. Not: Yes, she works.

          5 — The pattern

          Choose DO or DOES from the subject.

          SubjectQuestionYesNo
          IDo I work...?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
          youDo you work...?Yes, you do.No, you don't.
          heDoes he work...?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.

          itDoes it work...?Yes, it does.No, it doesn't.
          weDo we work...?Yes, we do.No, we don't.
          theyDo they work...?Yes, they do.No, they don't.

          DO -> I / YOU / WE / THEY

          DOES -> HE / SHE / IT

          Keep this visible.

          I / YOU / WE / THEY->DO + I / YOU / WE / THEY + BASE VERB?
